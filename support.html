Frequently asked questions

How do I set Mosaic as my default launcher?

Open Android Settings → Apps → Default apps → Home app, then select Mosaic Launcher. The exact path varies by device.

Why aren't notification previews appearing?

Check notification access in Android Settings and ensure notifications are enabled for the relevant apps. Available behavior can vary by device and Android version.

Can I customize tile sizes?

Mosaic is designed around a four-column grid with different tile sizes. Available controls depend on the installed version.

How do I report a bug?

Email [your support email] with your device model, Android version, app version, and steps to reproduce. Avoid including private notification content or sensitive information.
